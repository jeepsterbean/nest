## Design Context

Shared dashboard controls and cross-surface UI rules live in `UI.md`. Product language lives in `CONTEXT.md`; Section behavior lives
in the files `AGENTS.md` names. See `AGENTS.md` for the full map.

### Users
Nest is a private self-care tool for one person doing quick check-ins, managing
personal tasks, and capturing unstructured thoughts without interrupting their
flow.

### Brand Personality
Calm, warm, and focused. The interface should feel private and supportive
without becoming clinical, decorative, or overly playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined journal-like visual language with warm surfaces, restrained color,
compact controls, and clear hierarchy. Preserve light and dark system themes.

### Design Principles
- Keep reflection and capture actions immediately available.
- Prefer quiet progressive disclosure over modal or oversized controls.
- Use compact, familiar controls with accessible labels and focus states.
- Preserve user drafts when switching views or levels of detail.
- Keep information readable and responsive without crowding the workspace.
